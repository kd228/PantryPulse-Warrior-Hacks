# PantryPulse: 2:50 video script (v2, with the decision assistant)

Record with the pitch deck on screen, then switch to the live demo for the last section. Fill in the bracketed parts. About 410 words, which is roughly 2:45 at a calm pace.

## Slide 1, Cover (0:00–0:08)
Hi, we are [team name]. Our project is PantryPulse. Good food gets thrown out in one part of town while a pantry across town runs short of that exact food.

## Slides 2–3, Problem and who it affects (0:08–0:35)
USDA estimates that 30 to 40 percent of the US food supply is wasted. At the same time, pantries run short. The issue is timing, because fresh food spoils in hours, and mismatch, because pantries often get what was donated instead of what they need.
Donors want to give but have no time to call around. Pantries are volunteer-run with limited cold storage. And families miss the foods that are hardest to supply: fresh produce and dairy.

## Slides 4–6, Solution, flow and logic (0:35–1:08)
PantryPulse is a matching layer between surplus food and real need. A donor posts what they have and how long it stays safe. We rule out any pantry with no cold storage, any that cannot be reached before the food spoils, and any that close too soon. We score the rest out of 100: need, distance, time to spare, and space. Big donations split across pantries, and every ranking shows its reasons.

## Slide 7, Decision assistant (1:08–1:30)
To make decisions easier, we added an assistant. Quick questions, like why number one won, what was ruled out, or whether to split, are answered instantly from the matching engine. Typed questions go to Claude, which sees the live donation and rankings. It advises and the coordinator decides. It never rules on food safety.

## Slide 8, Live demo (1:30–2:20)
[Switch to the demo page.] Here is the working prototype. I pick the Grocery produce scenario: 180 pounds of fresh produce that stays safe for four hours. Eastside ranks first. The map shows distance to scale, and Riverbend is ruled out because it has no refrigeration.
Now I tap "Compare priorities" in the assistant. With a balanced view Eastside wins, but with "Neediest first" Northgate wins. That is the real tradeoff, and the assistant lays it out in one line.
[Tap "Cafeteria dairy".] 700 pounds of dairy splits across two pantries. [Click a cell in the need board.] If a pantry flags a shortage, the ranking updates right away. [Type "Is Hilltop a good backup?"] And I can just ask.

## Slides 9–11, Impact, feasibility, roadmap (2:20–2:42)
We would measure pounds rerouted, meals provided at 1.2 pounds per meal, time to match, and need fit. The main risks are food safety, stale data, adoption, privacy and assistant errors, and each has a plan. We start with a six-week pilot of three pantries and five donors, then add volunteer routing, then scale across the county.

## Slide 12, Close (2:42–2:50)
PantryPulse gets the same food to the right place in time. We would love to pilot it with a local food network. Thank you.
